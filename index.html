Best Community Project Award — Engineering Expo 2026

My capstone team developed an autonomous driving system for an existing electric golf cart, intended for controlled environments such as university campuses. The project integrated five subsystems: perception, Model Predictive Control (MPC), steering, braking and acceleration.

I developed and evaluated the lane detection, object detection and trajectory generation using HybridNets, YOLOv8n and Python, and helped with the physical assembly and electrical wiring.
