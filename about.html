My story

I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate from Bahrain Polytechnic (GPA 3.46/4.00). My path into engineering started with hands-on work: before university I earned a Diploma in Electrical Maintenance at Nasser Vocational Training Centre. That background still shapes how I design. I think about how a part will be made, assembled and maintained, not only how it looks on screen.

At university I focused on mechanical design and analysis. I have modelled gearboxes, vehicle chassis and machines in SOLIDWORKS, checked them with Finite Element Analysis, and helped turn those designs into working prototypes. In my capstone I also moved into software. I built the computer-vision perception system for an autonomous golf cart, and our team won the Best Community Project Award at the Engineering Expo 2026.

During industrial training at Gulf Petrochemical Industries Co. (GPIC), I worked with the Inspection and Mechanical Maintenance teams on NDT, permit-to-work and plant equipment such as pumps, valves, pressure vessels and heat exchangers. I am now completing the IOSH Level 3 Certificate in Occupational Safety and Health. My goal is a graduate mechanical engineering role where good design and safe practice go together.
